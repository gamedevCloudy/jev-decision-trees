A gain is the graph's match count minus the flat match count, on the shared denominator.

- Gain of 0 or 1: the flat call stays the system to use. The extra calls do not enter the claim.
- Gain of 2 or more: that graph stays in the paper, with its cost minus the flat cost in the same sentence.
- Loss of 2 or more: write that the handoff lowers the match count.
- If the chain and the dag and fact-chain all gain 2 or more, keep the cheapest of those three. The flat call stays in the table either way.
- Fact-chain may replace the chain in the claim only when its gain is at least 2, or when its match count equals the chain and its cost is lower. A tie with the flat call leaves the flat call in place.
